# Super-Store-Sales-Dashboard
This dashboard is a comprehensive visualization of retail sales data, designed to help stakeholders quickly understand performance across different regions, categories, and customer segments. I built it using Power BI, focusing on clarity, interactivity, and real-time insights.

What This Dashboard Shows:
Overall Business Performance At the top, key metrics like Total Sales ($1.6M), Quantity Sold (22K), Profit ($175K), and Returns (6K) give a quick snapshot of how the store is doing.

Sales by Shipping Method Users can see how different shipping modes (Standard, Second Class, First Class, Same Day) contribute to overall sales. This helps identify customer preferences and logistics efficiency.

Category & Sub-Category Breakdown Sales are broken down by major categories like Office Supplies, Furniture, and Technology, and further into sub-categories such as Phones, Chairs, Storage, and Tables. This helps pinpoint which products drive revenue.

Geographic Insights A map of the U.S. shows Profit and Sales by State, with bubble indicators for volume. A bar chart highlights top-performing states like California, Texas, and New York.

Customer Segmentation Donut charts show sales distribution by Segment (Consumer, Corporate, Home Office) and Payment Mode (Credit Card, Cash, COD), offering insights into buyer behavior.

Regional Performance Sales are split across South, East, West, and Central regions, helping businesses tailor strategies for each area.

Year-over-Year Trends Line charts compare Monthly Sales and Profit for 2019 vs. 2020, revealing seasonal patterns and growth areas.

15-Day Sales Forecast A predictive chart shows expected sales for the next 15 days, helping with inventory planning and goal setting.

Tools & Techniques Used:
Power BI for data modeling, DAX calculations, and interactive visuals

Custom visuals for maps, donut charts, and line graphs

Forecasting tools to predict short-term sales trends

Slicers and filters for dynamic region-based analysis
